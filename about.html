THE BAND

STARTHISTLE • REDDING, NORTHERN CALIFORNIA

ORIGIN

NO BACKING TRACKS.
JUST AMPS.

Based in Northern California, StarThistle revives the authoritative, gritty essence of the 90s.

Hailing from Redding, California, StarThistle delivers dynamic 90s covers alongside explosive, hard-hitting originals. No backing sequences and no artificial polish, just tube amps turned up and pure raw energy.

Rather than locking into one single narrow sound, StarThistle brings an eclectic plethora of 90s-rooted music to life, connecting with crowds through unfiltered passion and unforgettable live performances across Northern California.
